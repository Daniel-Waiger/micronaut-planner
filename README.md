# μicronaut Planner

A static, zero-install **microscopy experiment planner**. It walks you from a
research question through assays, panel, controls, and acquisition, and produces
a file-naming convention as a downstream artifact of a finished design.

**▶ Open the app:** https://daniel-waiger.github.io/micronaut-planner/

Everything runs in your browser — no upload, no install, no server, no account.
Your work is autosaved locally; use **Download** in the app to keep a copy.

> **Alpha.** This is an early preview shared with a small group for feedback.
> It's a planning aid, not a validated instrument model — verify settings at the
> scope. Guidance content is still being reviewed by a microscopy specialist.

This repository hosts only the built single-file app (`index.html`). It is a
compiled artifact; development happens in a separate repository.
